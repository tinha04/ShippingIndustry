## 2. Kill criteria

1. No insurer or broker would pay for risk data in 3 of 5 calls.
2. No capital partner will work with us within 12 months.
3. No liability path (treaty, national law or backstop) by 2029.

## 3. Open questions

1. What would Northcourt, AXA XL or NorthStandard need to quote a nuclear ship?
2. What does a refused or delayed port call cost a shipowner today?
3. Would a government backstop for ships look like Price-Anderson [8]?
4. Who insures floating plants moored in one port: marine or nuclear markets?
