## 1. One line

Risk data and scores that let insurers price nuclear ships and floating plants, a narrow first cover for port refusal or delay, and later a specialist underwriting agency with partner capital.

## 2. Is it doable and does it solve a pain?

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Real pain? | **Yes, the strongest of the three** | Nuclear excluded from almost all marine policies [3]; shipowner insurers' reinsurance excludes it [1]; $50M cap [2]; no treaty [4, 5]; Savannah needed a government guarantee [L24] |
| Doable by us? | **Not as an insurer, yet** | An underwriting agency needs licenses, underwriters and capital partners; incumbents hold them [11 to 15] |
| Open gap? | **Narrow** | Northcourt underwrites nuclear plants, Markel and Willis cover plant property, NorthStandard works in the Lloyd's Register consortium [11, 12, 16]. No ship focused startup found [19] |
| Who pays? | **Unproven** | Insurers and brokers for data; shipowners and floating plant developers for cover |

## 3. Product (H)

| Stage | Product | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Risk data (now)** | Risk score per ship, reactor and route: reactor type, readiness of each port on the route (idea 1), crew, security, monitoring (idea 2); a data room for underwriters | Public sources plus customer data; no license |
| **B. Port refusal or delay cover (the bet we are testing)** | Pays a fixed amount when a port refuses or holds a nuclear ship beyond an agreed time; priced from readiness scores | A capital partner; no nuclear capacity, because it covers lost time, not radiation damage |
| **C. Structured nuclear cover (later)** | Layers: shipowner insurer up to $50M [2], a nuclear liability layer from pools or Lloyd's [12, 14], and a government backstop like Price-Anderson [8] | Treaty or national law; capital partners |
| **D. Underwriting agency (later)** | Underwrite on behalf of capital partners, the Northcourt model [12] | License, underwriters, loss data |

**Tech (K):** risk model as transparent factor scores, not a black box, so underwriters can see why; data room with access logs; trigger data for stage B from port records and position data.

## 4. Boundary

- We do not sell insurance until licensed or acting for a licensed partner.
- Scores are inputs to an underwriter's decision, not a guarantee of safety.
- No loss data exists for nuclear merchant ships; every model is judgment until claims history builds (risk IN5).

## 5. Why port refusal cover first

Nuclear liability needs a treaty or national law and very large capital [4, 5, 8]. Port refusal or delay is a commercial risk a startup can model with readiness data, needs modest capital, and pays out on a clear event. It is also the risk shipowners face first: Otto Hahn needed diplomatic talks for each port [L23]. No product found (H).
