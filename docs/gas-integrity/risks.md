## 2. Kill criteria

1. Fewer than 3 of 8 ship managers or owners would pay $8K per ship a year.
2. Class requires a new type approval taking more than 12 months before a pilot.
3. Makers of most installed panels refuse data access and analog taps are not acceptable.

## 3. Open questions

1. How often do port state control and vetting cite gas detection today (2024 to 2026 data)?
2. What does a delay or detention cost a gas carrier or gas fueled ship per day?
3. Which panels publish Modbus maps?
4. Would a test gas supplier or Ofiniti partner on distribution?
