## 2. Kill criteria

1. Fewer than 3 of 10 buyers would pay for a port scorecard or route map.
2. A free tool from Lloyd's Register, NEMO or the IAEA covers per port scoring.
3. Our contact's project stalls and no other design partner signs within 6 months.

## 3. Open questions

1. What does our contact's project already know about its ports, and who did that work?
2. Do ports budget for nuclear readiness today, or only after a ship asks to call?
3. Would MARAD fund or adopt a common port scorecard?
4. What did the Rotterdam and Corpus Christi studies cost?
