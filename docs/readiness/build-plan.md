## 2. Build

| Step | Work | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rule map v0:** list every requirement a nuclear ship call triggers, from public sources: IMO 1981 code and SOLAS VIII, ABS nuclear requirements and nuclear ready guide, Lloyd's Register guidance, NRC and Coast Guard MOU, NRC proposed Part 57, IAEA program topics, Rotterdam study [L13 to L18, L6] | 100+ requirements, each with source clause, owner (flag, class, regulator, port) and voyage stage |
| 2 | **Port scorecard v0:** about 30 questions in 6 areas from the Rotterdam and Corpus Christi study scopes [L5, L6, 1]; score Long Beach, Corpus Christi and Rotterdam from public information | Our contact and one port say the scores match their own view |
| 3 | **Route view for our contact:** for their ship concept and intended route, each port's score, canals, bans and missing items | Contact says it saves weeks of advisor time, or names what is wrong |
| 4 | **Cited search:** answers rule questions from the public document set, citing the clause; says "not covered" when no source exists | At least 18 of 20 test questions answered with the correct clause; 0 answers without a source |
| 5 | **Paid pilot:** one port scorecard or project readiness file | A signed order at $50K or more |

## 3. Decision gate

After 10 interviews and step 3: promote to Strong candidate if at least 3 buyers would pay and our contact uses the route view. Otherwise move to Partner or license (offer the scorecard to NEMO, MARAD or a class society).
