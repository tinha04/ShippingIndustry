## 1. Problem (V)

- A nuclear ship needs approvals from flag, class, the nuclear regulator, coastal states and every port it calls, plus valid insurance and trained operators on ship and shore [1].
- History: Otto Hahn reached about 33 ports in 22 countries, each by diplomatic negotiation [L23]. New Zealand bans nuclear ships within 12 miles [L25].
- The Rotterdam study found the main barriers are regulatory alignment, governance, risk management and public acceptance, not technology [L6].
- Each port starts from zero: Rotterdam and Corpus Christi ran separate studies [L5, L6]. NEMO's practical port guide is due in 2027 [L19].

## 2. Why now (V)

The US plans 50 nuclear merchant ships with construction from 2028 [L2]; MARAD asked industry about port acceptance (closed August 5, 2026) [L1]; the IAEA launched ATLAS with 26 countries in August 2026 [L14]; the NRC and Coast Guard signed a new MOU and an NRC maritime paper is due this fall [L15]; the IMO code rewrite targets 2030 [L13]; three early design approvals since June 2026 [L11]; a US to South Korea nuclear car carrier study was signed this month [L9].

## 4. Buyers

| Buyer | Why they pay | Concentration |
|---|---|---|
| Ship and floating plant projects (owners, reactor developers, shipyards) | Know which routes and ports are possible before ordering a ship | Medium: about 12 reactor developers, a few yards, a few owners |
| Ports | Decide whether and how to host nuclear ships; win future calls | Medium: dozens of candidate ports |
| MARAD and other governments | Public programs need a repeatable port method | High: few buyers |
| Insurers | Readiness evidence for pricing (stage 3) | High |

## 5. Read on venture scale

Readiness alone reaches tens of millions a year at most, and only once ships are in design. A $100M path needs stages 2 and 3 on the same record ([portfolio](../portfolio.md#2-how-the-three-ideas-fit-proposal)). Its role is the first sale and the data base for the rest.

## 6. Assumptions to test

1. Ports will pay for a scorecard before any nuclear ship has asked to call.
2. Projects will pay for a route map before ordering a ship.
3. A shared record beats each party keeping its own files.
4. Free guidance (NEMO, Lloyd's Register, IAEA) leaves room for a paid tool.
5. Our contact's project will act as a design partner.
